About

I'm Roey Leon, and I make things by hand. My workshop turns out cutting boards, furniture and pieces for kids, as well as three-dimensional wall reliefs in plaster, render and paint.

Every piece starts as raw material and goes through every stage with me: choosing the wood, cutting, gluing, sculpting the layers, sanding and finishing. There's no production line here, and no two pieces are alike.

If a piece speaks to you, or you have an idea for a particular space, I'd love to hear about it.
