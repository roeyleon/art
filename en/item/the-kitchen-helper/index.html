More about this piece

The legs splay outward in an A-frame, so the base is wider than the top and stays especially stable even when the child moves around. A low step at the front leads up to a platform at counter height.
The frame is painted yellow, and the platforms are solid pine in its natural tone.

Every tower is built from solid wood, a beech frame with pine platforms, and sanded smooth so it's pleasant for small hands.
Important: a learning tower is meant to be used under adult supervision.
